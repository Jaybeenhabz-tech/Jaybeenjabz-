# Gender-based violence — resources and support

This document provides basic information, immediate steps, and links to get help. It is intended to be a respectful, non-triggering summary and a place to list localized resources.

## Immediate safety
- If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services right away.
- If you are not in immediate danger but need urgent support, contact a local or national hotline (see resources below).

## How to help someone safely
- Believe them and listen without judgment.
- Respect their privacy and let them choose next steps.
- Encourage professional support (medical, legal, counseling) and offer to help connect them.
- Avoid pressuring them to disclose details they are not ready to share.
